# reef-propagation-log

*(working title — may rename once the research topic is locked in December 2026)*

Open logbook of a self-built ~50cm marine reef microcosm, kept by a high school student in Jiangsu, China. Recording setup, water chemistry, candidate research topics, and results — including failures — as they happen.

## Who
A high-school student in Jiangsu, China, who has kept aquariums for several years. US applicant, planning to study environmental / ecological engineering.

## Why
Around 90–95% of marine ornamental species in the aquarium trade are still taken from the wild, because captive breeding is difficult for most of them. This tank started as an attempt to work on the propagation side of that problem, and has grown into a testbed for a real research project — not just fishkeeping.

## Current status — 2026-08-28

Main tank is built and cycling; nothing running at project scale yet.

- **Tank:** ~50cm cube, sump-based with refugium, ~100–120L. Cycling started 2026-08-12; aquascaping complete 2026-08-24.
- **Old tank (50×30×30, ~37.5L):** kept as quarantine/backup only, no longer part of the main project.
- **Livestock:** 4 clownfish (some may move into the new tank), 1 sea cucumber, 1 small zoanthid frag.
- **Research topic:** not locked yet. Weighing about 10 candidate directions this fall — final call by end of December 2026.

## Monitoring — a real limitation, stated plainly

Test kits are range/color-band type — reading a band, not a precise number. Currently have working reagents for NH3 and KH only (no NO2/NO3/PO4). NH3 has read consistently near-colorless across several tests; haven't yet confirmed whether that means the tank is genuinely stable or the reagent itself has gone bad. A positive-control test is next.

## Retired experiment

**E01 — light reduction vs. nuisance algae** (started 2026-07-19, old 37.5L tank): reduced light to 40% intensity / 6h photoperiod to address heavy nuisance algae. Uncontrolled diagnostic, not a real experiment. Retired when the project moved to the new tank.
